Experimental browser sync for Pro users. Uses your logged-in claude.ai / chatgpt.com session in this browser, then posts only chat metadata (id, title, link, times) to Desk. Session cookies never leave your browser for Anthropic/OpenAI.

How it works

  1. Stay logged into claude.ai and/or chatgpt.com in this browser.
  2. Point Desk URL at your Desk (local or hosted).
  3. Chats are listed in-page and pushed to POST /api/sync/import. Desk auto-pulls them onto the board.
